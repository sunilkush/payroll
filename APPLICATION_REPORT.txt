1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SYSTEM OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This project is an enterprise-grade multi-tenant HRMS, Attendance, Field Sales, 
and Payroll Management System designed for small-to-large businesses. The system 
eliminates attendance fraud using location-verified geofencing, provides multiple 
daily punch capabilities with live net work tracking, automates statutory 
Indian payroll calculations (EPF, EPS, ESI, TDS) on a standard 30-day basis, 
and supports client field visits with selfie and QR proof.

The application is deployed across three environments:
1. Backend REST API: Node.js (ES Modules) + Express + MongoDB (Mongoose)
2. Web Management Portal: React 18 + Vite + Ant Design + Leaflet Maps (PWA)
3. Mobile Application: React Native (Expo) + Leaflet WebView Maps

4. CORE MODULES & WORKFLOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 AUTHENTICATION & COMPANY SELF-REGISTRATION
   - Self-registration with 10 active staff free plan allocation.
   - Email verification system with 6-digit cryptographic security codes.
   - Password reset workflow with expiring single-use reset tokens.
   - Multi-device login support with token revocation on password change.

4.2 SMART OFFICE ATTENDANCE & MULTI-PUNCH GEOFENCE ENGINE
   - Office Timing: Default 09:30 AM to 06:30 PM (Asia/Kolkata timezone).
   - Required Net Working Hours: Configurable, default 8 hours 30 minutes (510 min).
   - Lunch Break: Configurable, default 30 minutes (excluded from working hours).
   - Multiple Punching:
     * Staff can punch in and out multiple times during the day.
     * First valid punch of the day is official "First In".
     * Last valid punch of the day is official "Last Out".
     * Net working hours are calculated by strictly summing completed [IN -> OUT]
       intervals. Intermediate break periods are excluded.
     * Lunch is never deducted twice: If an employee punches out for a 30+ minute
       lunch break, the interval calculation already excludes it.
   - Half-Day Attendance Policy (4:30 PM Cutoff):
     * If an employee completes 8h 30m net working time (before or after 4:30 PM),
       attendance is marked PRESENT.
     * At 4:30 PM, if the employee is still punched in and working, the system
       displays "Half Day Pending / Eligible" and DOES NOT prematurely finalize
       Half-Day while they are still working.
     * If the employee punches out at or after 4:30 PM with less than 8h 30m worked,
       Half-Day is finalized.
   - Geofence & GPS Validation:
     * Validates employee GPS latitude, longitude, and accuracy against all
       authorized company office branches.
     * Enforces company `maxGpsAccuracy` (default 100m).
     * Detailed audit logs recorded in `PunchAudit` for both accepted and rejected
       attempts (reason, coordinates, IP, user-agent).
   - Real-Time Attendance Dashboard:
     * Live ticking net-work counter (accumulated completed + current open interval).
     * Displays First In, Last Out, Total Working Hours, Break Time, Required Hours,
       Remaining Hours, Punch Count, and Attendance Status.

4.3 LEAVE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
   - Configurable monthly quotas for Casual Leave (CL) and Earned Leave (EL).
   - Automatic monthly leave credit job (runs on 1st of each month).
   - Half-day leave support: First Half or Second Half.
   - Year-end EL carry-forward with configurable ceiling (`elCarryForwardMax`).
   - Full approval workflow (Pending -> Approved / Rejected).
   - Approved leaves automatically integrate into monthly attendance and payroll.

4.4 PUNCH CORRECTION REQUEST WORKFLOW
   - Staff can request corrections for missed punches (In time, Out time, or both).
   - Requires employee justification note.
   - Admin can approve or reject with comments.
   - Approved corrections update the attendance record with `corrected: true` audit tag.

4.5 FIELD SALES MEETINGS & CLIENT VISIT TRACKING
   - Admin schedules meetings with client details, location coordinates, and radius.
   - Sales manager checks in via Geofence + Dynamic QR code scan.
   - Configurable check-in window (grace minutes before start and after end).
   - Photo verification: Sales manager takes client site selfie to complete visit.
   - Follow-up meeting creation and CRM integration.
   - Automated cron job flags unvisited meetings as MISSED.

4.6 ENTERPRISE PAYROLL CALCULATION ENGINE
   - Fixed 30-Day Basis Convention:
     * Per Day Rate = Monthly Component Amount / 30
     * Payable Days = Math.max(0, 30 - LOP Days)
     * Earned Component = (Monthly Structure * Payable Days) / 30
   - Loss of Pay (LOP) integration:
     * LOP Days = Unapproved Absents + Pre-joining un-employed days + 0.5 * Half-Day.
     * 0 LOP in any month (28, 30, or 31 days) yields full monthly salary.
     * In February (28 days), full-month absence results in 0 payable days (not 2).
   - Salary Structure Breakdown:
     * Basic, HRA, Special Allowance, Other Allowance -> Gross Salary.
   - Other Deductions Guardrail:
     * Admin can enter custom deductions (advances, fines, loans).
     * System prevents negative net salary: Max deduction capped at (Earned Gross - PF - ESI).

4.7 STATUTORY COMPLIANCE: PF & ESI
   - Provident Fund (EPF / EPS):
     * Complies with Indian Labour Codes 50% wage floor rule:
       PF Wage = Math.max(Earned Basic, 50% of Earned Gross).
     * Optional Rs. 15,000 ceiling cap (`pfCapAtCeiling`).
     * Employee Contribution: 12% of PF Wage.
     * Employer Contribution: 12% total, split into EPS (8.33% up to ceiling, max Rs. 1,250)
       and EPF (balance).
     * EDLI (0.50%) and Admin Charges (0.50%) calculated for company audit.
   - Employee State Insurance (ESIC):
     * Threshold: Monthly Gross <= Rs. 21,000.
     * 6-Month Contribution Period Lock: Once covered in April-Sept or Oct-March,
       coverage continues until period end regardless of mid-period increments.
     * Employee Contribution: 0.75% of Earned Gross (rounded up to next rupee).
     * Employer Contribution: 3.25% of Earned Gross (rounded up to next rupee).

4.8 EXPORT SERVICES: PDF SLIPS & EXCEL
   - Salary Slips (PDFKit): Clean corporate layout with company letterhead, employee
     statutory details (UAN, ESIC, Bank), attendance summary, earnings vs deductions,
     net pay highlight, and employer statutory contribution note.
   - Bank/CA Export (ExcelJS): Comprehensive spreadsheet with all 31 employee fields,
     formatted currency values (`#,##0.00`), and automatic column sum formulas.

5. DATABASE ARCHITECTURE & COLLECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. `companies`: Company profile, plan, branch locations with geofence radius,
   paid holidays, and operational settings (PF, ESI, working hours, week-offs).
2. `users`: Authentication credentials, roles, employee profile, bank details,
   UAN, ESIC, salary structure, and status.
3. `attendances`: Daily record per staff (`userId + date` unique index). Stores
   first `punchIn`, last `punchOut`, permanent `punches` array, `netWorkMinutes`,
   and `status` (PRESENT, HALF_DAY, ABSENT).
4. `punchaudits`: Immutable audit trail for every punch attempt (coordinates,
   timestamp, accuracy, status, rejection reason, IP, user-agent).
5. `leaves`: Employee leave applications (CL, EL, half-day), dates, approval status.
6. `punchcorrections`: Correction requests with admin review status.
7. `clients`: Business clients directory with addresses and locations.
8. `meetings`: Sales client meetings, coordinates, QR tokens, photos, and evidence.
9. `payrolls`: Monthly payroll records (`userId + period` unique index). Stores
   snapshot of staff profile, earnings, deductions, PF/ESI breakdown, and net salary.
10. `settings`: Global system settings (PF ceiling, ESI limit, rates, plan limits).

7. SECURITY, FRAUD PREVENTION & AUDITING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Server-Side Trusted Timestamps:
   - Attendance timestamps and payroll computations are strictly generated on
     the backend using trusted UTC/IST time. Client-side timestamps are ignored.
2. Server-Side Geofence Verification:
   - Location verification is calculated on the server using Haversine formulas.
   - Fake GPS and device coordinate manipulation cannot bypass server geofencing.
3. Punch Audit Trail:
   - Every punch attempt (success or rejection) is permanently recorded in the
     `PunchAudit` collection with GPS accuracy, distance from nearest office,
     IP address, and user-agent string.
4. Database Concurrency & Race-Condition Safety:
   - Unique compound indexes (`userId + date`) prevent duplicate attendance
     records. Atomic updates prevent concurrent double-punch race conditions.
5. Invariable Historical Payroll Slips:
   - Generating payroll creates an immutable snapshot of employee details.
     Subsequent employee salary increments or banking edits do not corrupt past slips.
